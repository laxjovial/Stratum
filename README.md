# Stratum: The Self-Healing Organizational Operating System 🌌

> **"Organization is the art of getting things done through others. Stratum is the engine that masters that art."**

Stratum is not just another project management tool or a HR dashboard. It is a **Hierarchical Intelligence Layer** designed to act as the central nervous system for a modern workforce. Whether you are a solo entrepreneur mimicking a 50-person team to automate your scale, or a CEO of a global enterprise, Stratum provides the structural integrity and AI-driven insights to ensure your vision is executed without friction.

---

## 🏛️ The Vision: Why Stratum?

In most organizations, information flows like water in a broken pipe: it leaks, it pools in the wrong places (silos), and the people at the top often lose sight of what’s happening at the bottom. 

Stratum solves this by enforcing a **Digital Hierarchy** where power and data are logically linked. It is built on three core pillars:

### 1. Superior Priority Control (The Power Layer)
Stratum introduces the concept of **Inherited Data Rights**. 
- **Superiors** automatically possess "Priority View" over the work, lessons, and documents created by their subordinates. 
- **Subordinates** operate in a "Safe Sandbox" where they can create and manage their own assets, but cannot overstep organizational boundaries.
- **The Result**: A secure, transparent reporting line where accountability is built into the code itself.

### 2. The Organizational Web (The Visualization Layer)
Traditional org charts are static PDFs that gather dust. Stratum's **Neural Web** is a live, interactive map of your workforce. 
- **Live Reconfiguration**: Drag-and-drop reporting lines to restructure your company in real-time.
- **Neural Connections**: See how departments interact and where communication bottlenecks exist.

### 3. The Robotic Butler (The Intelligence Layer)
Our RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline isn't just a chatbot; it's a **Structural Auditor**.
- **Gap Detection**: The AI analyzes your organizational web and document flow to identify missing training (Lessons) or "lonely" employees with no clear supervisor.
- **Proactive Intervention**: Instead of waiting for you to ask, the Butler suggests: *"I notice the Sales team is struggling with the new S3 integration documentation. Should I generate a Lesson for them?"*

## 🔐 The Onboarding Protocol (The "Stratum Key")

Stratum follows a "Invite-Only" security model to prevent unauthorized access:
1. **Initiation**: An authorized Admin/HR enters a candidate's email and pre-assigns their Role and Department.
2. **The Stratum Key**: The system generates a unique, one-time cryptographic token sent via email.
3. **Claiming Identity**: The staff member uses their email + token to sign up. They are instantly bound to their pre-assigned place in the hierarchy.
4. **Transition**: Upon first login, they set their permanent password and enter the workforce.

## 🛠️ Technical Ecosystem

Stratum is a monorepo built for high performance and provider-agnosticism:
- **Backend**: FastAPI (Python) - The "Superior Priority" Engine.
- **Frontend**: Next.js (React/TypeScript) - The Command Center.
- **Intelligence**: RAG Pipeline (LangChain/Gemini) - The Robotic Butler.
- **Database**: PostgreSQL (Supabase/pgvector) - The Memory.
- **Storage**: AWS S3 (with Supabase fallback) - The Vault.
